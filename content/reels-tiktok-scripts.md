# Reels & TikTok scripts — EN / FR / IT / ES

Six short-form concepts for `Roastin Has Notes`, designed for 15–25 second vertical videos. Each concept can be filmed by one creator with a phone, a screen recording and simple jump cuts.

## Production rules

- Show the result or the tension in the first two seconds.
- Keep captions on screen throughout.
- Never show a real private conversation without every participant's permission; use synthetic chat fixtures by default.
- Do not present synthetic reactions as testimonials.
- End on the report preview or the brand lockup, not on a generic AI interface.
- Core visual rhythm: face camera → synthetic chat → report reveal → reaction → CTA.

## 1. I made the mistake of uploading the group chat

**Angle:** Product discovery / curiosity  
**Length:** 18–22 seconds  
**Shot list:** shocked face → synthetic group chat → report scroll → reaction

### English

**0–2s — Hook, face camera**  
“I made the mistake of letting Roastin read our group chat.”

**2–7s — Screen recording**  
“He found the friend who says ‘five minutes away’ while still at home…”

**7–12s — Reveal one report card**  
“…the one who starts drama and then replies with a single skull emoji…”

**12–17s — Back to face camera**  
“…and apparently I’m the unpaid customer support of the group.”

**17–21s — Brand + CTA**  
“Upload the chat. Roastin has notes.”  
On-screen: `Let Roastin read it.`

### Français

**0–2 s — Accroche, face caméra**  
« J’ai fait l’erreur de laisser Roastin lire notre groupe WhatsApp. »

**2–7 s — Capture d’écran**  
« Il a repéré celui qui dit “j’arrive” alors qu’il est encore chez lui… »

**7–12 s — Révélation d’une carte du rapport**  
« …celle qui lance un débat puis disparaît après un seul emoji tête de mort… »

**12–17 s — Retour face caméra**  
« …et apparemment, moi, je suis le service après-vente gratuit du groupe. »

**17–21 s — Marque + CTA**  
« Importe la conversation. Roastin a pris des notes. »  
À l’écran : `Laisse Roastin la lire.`

### Italiano

**0–2 s — Gancio, camera frontale**  
« Ho commesso l’errore di far leggere a Roastin la nostra chat di gruppo. »

**2–7 s — Registrazione schermo**  
« Ha trovato quello che scrive “arrivo tra cinque minuti” ed è ancora a casa… »

**7–12 s — Una scheda del report**  
« …quella che scatena il caos e poi risponde solo con un teschio… »

**12–17 s — Reazione in camera**  
« …e a quanto pare io sono l’assistenza clienti gratuita del gruppo. »

**17–21 s — Brand + CTA**  
« Carica la chat. Roastin ha preso appunti. »

### Español

**0–2 s — Gancho, a cámara**  
« Cometí el error de dejar que Roastin leyera nuestro grupo de WhatsApp. »

**2–7 s — Grabación de pantalla**

## 2. Tag the friend who does this

**Angle:** Comments and tagging  
**Length:** 15–18 seconds  
**Shot list:** creator plays four roles with fast jump cuts

### English

**0–2s — Hook**  
“Every group chat has these four people.”

**2–11s — Four cuts**  
“The planner nobody listens to.”  
“The ghost who returns with 63 unread messages.”  
“The one who only sends voice notes.”  
“And the person who screenshots everything.”

**11–15s — Punchline**  
“Roastin will tell you which one you are. Your friends already know.”

**15–18s — CTA**  
“Tag the group chat suspect.”

“That is still support.”

**23–25s — CTA**  
“Settle your group chat arguments.”

**2–8s — Face camera**

“Roastin gave them: ‘Most Likely to Say “Long Story Short” and Lie.’”

**13–18s — CTA**  
“Send this to your group chat broadcaster.”

## 5. Couples: the evidence was in the chat

**Angle:** Couple banter without judging the relationship  
**Length:** 18–22 seconds  
**Shot list:** couple disagreement → synthetic receipts → verdict → laugh

### English

**0–3s — Hook**  
“He says I’m the dramatic one, so we checked the evidence.”

**3–9s — Three synthetic messages on screen**  
“Me: ‘What do you want for dinner?’”  
“Him: ‘Anything.’”  
“Also him: rejects seven options.”

**9–16s — Roastin report**  
“Roastin’s note: ‘He doesn’t want anything. He wants you to guess the one correct thing.’”

**16–20s — Partner reaction**  
“Okay, but pizza would’ve been fine.”

**20–22s — CTA**  
“Bring receipts to your next debate.”

### Français

**0–3 s — Accroche**  
« Il dit que c’est moi qui dramatise, donc on a vérifié les preuves. »

**3–9 s — Trois faux messages à l’écran**  
« Moi : “Tu veux manger quoi ?” »  
« Lui : “Comme tu veux.” »  
« Toujours lui : refuse sept idées. »

**9–16 s — Note de Roastin**  
« Roastin a noté : “Il ne veut pas n’importe quoi. Il veut que tu devines l’unique bonne réponse.” »

**16–20 s — Réaction du partenaire**  
« Oui, mais pizza, ça m’allait. »

**20–22 s — CTA**  
« Sortez les preuves au prochain débat. »

### Italiano

**0–3 s — Gancio**  
« Dice che quella drammatica sono io, quindi abbiamo controllato le prove. »

**3–9 s — Tre messaggi sintetici sullo schermo**  
« Io: “Cosa vuoi mangiare?” »  
« Lui: “Per me è uguale.” »  
« Sempre lui: boccia sette proposte. »

**9–16 s — Nota di Roastin**  
« Roastin ha scritto: “Non vuole una cosa qualsiasi. Vuole che tu indovini l’unica risposta giusta.” »

**16–20 s — Reazione del partner**  
« Però la pizza andava bene. »

**20–22 s — CTA**

### Español

**0–3 s — Gancho**  
« Dice que la dramática soy yo, así que revisamos las pruebas. »

**3–9 s — Tres mensajes sintéticos en pantalla**  
« Yo: “¿Qué quieres cenar?” »  
« Él: “Me da igual.” »  
« También él: rechaza siete opciones. »

## 6. The privacy objection

**Angle:** Trust / objection handling  
**Length:** 18–22 seconds  
**Shot list:** skeptical reaction → three clean text cards → report preview → CTA

### English

**0–3s — Hook**  
“Upload my private group chat? Absolutely not.”

**3–10s — Three text cards**  
“Then I read how it works.”  
“Encrypted while the report is created.”  
“The original conversation is permanently deleted after generation.”

**10–16s — Show synthetic report preview**  
“So Roastin keeps the notes, not the chat.”

**16–21s — CTA**  
“Your chat stays yours. The roast is yours to share.”

### Français

**0–3 s — Accroche**  
« Importer mon groupe privé ? Certainement pas. »

**3–10 s — Trois cartes texte**  
« Puis j’ai regardé comment ça fonctionne. »  
« La conversation est chiffrée pendant la création du rapport. »  
« Le fichier d’origine est définitivement supprimé après la génération. »

**10–16 s — Montrer un aperçu synthétique**  
« Roastin garde donc ses notes, pas la conversation. »

**16–21 s — CTA**  
« Ton chat reste le tien. À toi de partager le roast. »

### Italiano

**0–3 s — Gancio**  
« Caricare la mia chat privata? Assolutamente no. »

**3–10 s — Tre schede di testo**  
« Poi ho letto come funziona. »  
« La chat è cifrata mentre viene creato il report. »  
« La conversazione originale viene eliminata definitivamente dopo la generazione. »

**10–16 s — Mostrare un’anteprima sintetica**  
« Quindi Roastin conserva gli appunti, non la chat. »

**16–21 s — CTA**  
« La chat resta tua. Il roast puoi condividerlo tu. »

### Español

**0–3 s — Gancho**  
« ¿Subir mi grupo privado? Ni hablar. »

**3–10 s — Tres tarjetas de texto**  
« Luego leí cómo funciona. »  
« El chat se cifra mientras se crea el informe. »  
« La conversación original se elimina definitivamente después de generarlo. »

**10–16 s — Mostrar una vista previa sintética**  
« Así que Roastin guarda sus apuntes, no el chat. »

**16–21 s — CTA**  
« Tu chat sigue siendo tuyo. Tú decides si compartes el roast. »

## Suggested publishing order

1. `The voice-note criminal` — broadest relatable hook.
2. `Every group chat has these four people` — strongest tagging mechanic.
3. `I made the mistake of uploading the group chat` — clearest product reveal.
4. `Who carries the friendship?` — ideal for duets and creator pairs.
5. `Couples: the evidence was in the chat` — expands the use case.
6. `The privacy objection` — retargeting and trust-building creative.

For each concept, test two openings: spoken face-camera and the punchline displayed as text before the creator appears. Keep the body identical so hook performance can be compared cleanly.
